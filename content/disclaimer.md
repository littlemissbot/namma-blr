This site compiles publicly available information about infrastructure projects in
Bengaluru from government budget documents, agency reports, audit reports and
published news coverage. Every figure is shown with its source and the date it was
recorded.

Figures are drawn from official documents and media reports as published. They are
not independently audited by us. Budget allocations, sanctioned amounts, funds
released and actual expenditure are different things, and we label which is which. An
allocation is not evidence that money was spent, and a delay is not evidence of
wrongdoing.

Project timelines change. Information is accurate as of the "verified on" date shown
for each project and may be outdated. We update this site quarterly.

We do not allege misconduct by any individual, official, department or contractor.
Infrastructure delays have many causes, including land acquisition, litigation,
clearances, funding, contractor capacity and design changes.

If you are an agency or individual and believe something here is inaccurate, write to
[email]. We will review it against our sources and publish a correction where
warranted. Corrections are logged publicly.

# RTI draft: GBA ward boundary files

**Purpose:** obtain official, machine-readable ward boundaries for the five GBA
corporations (Plan 2). This is the primary-source alternative to third-party
ward files.
**Status:** draft, not yet filed.

## How to file

- **Send the same application to all three authorities below.** Any of them
  may hold the files. Under Section 6(3), a PIO who receives a request for
  information held by another authority must transfer it there within 5 days,
  so a misdirected copy isn't wasted.
  1. Public Information Officer, **Greater Bengaluru Authority** (GBA)
  2. Public Information Officer, **Urban Development Department**, Government
     of Karnataka
  3. Public Information Officer, **Karnataka State Remote Sensing
     Applications Centre** (KSRSAC), which runs the KGIS portal
- **Fee:** ₹10 per application under the Karnataka RTI Rules, by the payment
  modes the department accepts (IPO, DD or cash). If the department accepts
  online RTI, file online. Before filing, confirm the current fee and whether
  there are charges for copying to CD or DVD.
- **Keep the proof of filing.** The PIO must reply within 30 days
  (Section 7(1)). If there's no reply or the reply is inadequate, a first
  appeal goes to the First Appellate Authority in the same department within
  30 days.
- **When the reply arrives:** add it as a source with
  `doc_type: "rti_response"` and place the files under
  `data/wards/gba2025/source/`, as described in Plan 2.

## Application text

> To,
> The Public Information Officer,
> [Authority name and address]
>
> **Subject: Request for information under Section 6(1) of the Right to
> Information Act, 2005: ward boundaries of the corporations under the
> Greater Bengaluru Authority**
>
> Sir/Madam,
>
> I request the following information:
>
> 1. A copy of the final notification(s) delimiting the wards of the city
>    corporations constituted under the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act,
>    2024, including all schedules listing ward numbers, ward names and the
>    boundary description of each ward.
> 2. The digital boundary files (shapefile, KML, GeoJSON or any other GIS
>    format in which they are held) showing the boundary of every ward in
>    each of these corporations, as used for the final notification in item 1.
> 3. The coordinate reference system or projection used in the files in
>    item 2, and the date on which those files were last updated.
> 4. Any table held by the authority that maps each new ward to the former
>    BBMP ward(s) it covers, whether 198-ward or 243-ward.
> 5. If the files in item 2 are held for the corporation boundaries but not
>    for individual wards, those corporation boundary files.
>
> Under Section 7(9), I request that items 2, 4 and 5 be provided in
> **electronic form, in the format in which they are held**, by email to
> [email address] or on CD/DVD. Printed maps are not a substitute for the
> digital files.
>
> If any of this information is held by another public authority, please
> transfer this application to that authority under Section 6(3) and inform
> me.
>
> I am a citizen of India. The application fee of ₹10 is paid by [mode,
> reference number].
>
> Name: [ ]
> Address for correspondence: [ ]
> Email: [ ]
> Phone: [ ]
> Date: [ ]
>
> Signature

## Before filing, confirm

- [ ] **Name of the Act.** It's stated as the "Greater Bengaluru Governance
      Act, 2024", from memory. Confirm the exact title and year against the
      Gazette.
- [ ] **Fee and payment modes.** Check each authority's RTI page.
- [ ] **Who signs.** Decide whether to file as an individual or as the
      project. The applicant's name becomes public if the reply is published.
      Publishing the RTI reply as a source is the plan.
